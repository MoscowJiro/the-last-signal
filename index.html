THE PREMISE

In 2041, an abandoned North Atlantic oceanographic station begins transmitting again after seventeen years of silence.

A three-person investigation team is sent to NAOS-06, a remote research facility officially abandoned after a structural failure in 2007.

Inside, they discover that parts of the station are still powered — and that its deepest acoustic monitoring system is receiving a signal identical to one recorded seventeen years earlier.

There is only one problem.

The original signal was received before it was transmitted.

AI supported a directed visual process. Each image was assessed for architecture, materials, light, scale, continuity and narrative purpose.

Visually striking images were rejected when they broke the world’s rules.
